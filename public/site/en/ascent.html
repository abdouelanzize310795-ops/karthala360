11 km · ≈ 1,970 m of ascent

0.0 km370 mMvouni

Setting off from the village

Fields and villages: bananas, coconut palms, cassava, mango trees, cloves, vanilla. A landscape shaped by the people who live here, crossed on foot from Mvouni.

  • Full Climb: starts on foot
  • Other tours: start by 4×4

4.9 km1,300 mOld camp

Into the cloud forest

Above the strawberry guava thickets, the dense rainforest: large endemic trees, tree ferns, orchids, mosses and lichens. The heart of the mountain’s biodiversity, and home to the Karthala scops owl.

  • Full Climb: about half of the ascent

7.3 km1,600 m4×4 stop

The 4×4 stops, the walk begins

For the Soft Trek and the Bivouac, the 4×4 stops at about 1,600 m. About two hours of walking remain to the caldera. For the Bivouac, the camp is always set up outside the caldera.

  • Soft Trek · Bivouac: walking starts here

8.7 km≈ 1,830 mEnd of concrete

The high heather

The shrubby savannah of giant heather (Philippia comoriensis): home to the Karthala white-eye, a bird found nowhere else.

  • Zero Effort: the 4×4 continues on the upper track

11.1 km2,300 mCaldera

Into the caldera

At the end of the path, the caldera: about 3 km by 4, a set of nested collapses, with the Choungou Chahalé at its centre. Zero Effort reaches it after about 25 minutes of walking.

  • Every tour arrives here
