Zero Effort

Effort: Easy

The crater without the effort: ideal for families or if you are short on time.

The 4×4 drops you near the top. A short walk across the caldera takes you to the crater rim.

Duration
1 day
Walking
≈ 25 min
Price
€350 for 1 to 4 people, then €50 per extra person (i.e. €175 pp for 2, €88 for 4)
Departures
Every day, on request, at least 48 hours in advance
Group
Private group, 1 to 4 people for the flat rate (more with a supplement)
Suggested contribution
€10 per person, optional
Minimum age
5 years

Access: 4×4 up the mountain (about 10 km from Mvouni), then about 25 minutes’ walk across the caldera

Who is it for: Families, people not used to walking, visitors short on time.

Included

  • Local guide and safety briefing
  • Return transport from Moroni
  • 4×4 from Mvouni (about 10 km each way)
  • Water for the day

Typical day

  • 7:00 am: pick-up at your hotel in Moroni
  • About 2 hours by 4×4 from Mvouni
  • About 25 minutes’ walk across the caldera to the crater rim
  • Break at the top
  • Back in Moroni around 3 pm

Times are indicative and adjusted to the weather and the group’s pace.

Soft Trek

Effort: Easy to moderate

A real two-hour hike, without the full climb.

The 4×4 covers the longest part of the track. You walk the last two hours to the caldera.

Duration
1 day
Walking
≈ 2 h
Price
€250 for 1 to 4 people, then €50 per extra person (i.e. €125 pp for 2, €63 for 4)
Departures
Every day, on request, at least 48 hours in advance
Group
Private group, 1 to 4 people for the flat rate (more with a supplement)
Suggested contribution
€7 per person, optional
Minimum age
8 years

Access: 4×4 for about 7.5 km from Mvouni to the 4×4 stop (around 1,600 m), then about 2 hours’ walk

Who is it for: Occasional walkers who want a real hike without the full ascent.

Included

  • Local guide and safety briefing
  • Return transport from Moroni
  • 4×4 from Mvouni (about 7.5 km each way)
  • Water for the day

Typical day

  • 6:00 am: pick-up at your hotel in Moroni
  • About 1 h 30 by 4×4 from Mvouni
  • About 2 hours’ walk to the caldera
  • Break and discovery of the caldera
  • Walk back down to the 4×4, back in Moroni around 4 pm

Times are indicative and adjusted to the weather and the group’s pace.

Volcano Bivouac

Effort: Moderate to hard

A night above the clouds and sunrise over the caldera.

Two days on the mountain, a night in a tent, sunset and sunrise above the caldera.

Duration
2 days, 1 night
Walking
≈ 2 h on day 1
Price
€150 per person
Departures
Every day, on request, at least 48 hours in advance
Group
From 2 people, price per person
Suggested contribution
€5 per person, optional
Minimum age
12 years

Access: 4×4 from Mvouni, then about 2 hours’ walk to the camp. The equipment is carried by the team

Who is it for: Fit walkers who want to experience the mountain at night.

Included

  • Local guide and safety briefing
  • Return transport from Moroni
  • Tent and sleeping bag
  • Dinner and breakfast
  • Water for the trip

Typical day

  • Day 1, 1:00 pm: pick-up at your hotel in Moroni
  • 4×4 from Mvouni, then about 2 hours’ walk
  • Camp set up before sunset, dinner
  • Day 2: sunrise over the caldera, breakfast
  • Discovery of the caldera, walk down, back in Moroni around 1 pm

Times are indicative and adjusted to the weather and the group’s pace.

Full Climb

For trained hikers Effort: Hard

The whole of Karthala, from the rainforest to the crater rim.

The full climb on foot: rainforest, high heathland, then the caldera. Nearly 2,000 metres of ascent.

Duration
1 day
Walking
≈ 6 h up
Price
€90 per person
Departures
Every day, on request, at least 48 hours in advance
Group
From 2 people, price per person
Suggested contribution
€2 per person, optional
Minimum age
15 years

Access: Full ascent on foot from Mvouni (about 370 m) to the caldera (about 2,300 m), about 11 km

Who is it for: Trained hikers used to long days of walking.

Included

  • Local guide and safety briefing
  • Return transport from Moroni
  • Water, juice and snack

Typical day

  • 4:30 am: pick-up at your hotel in Moroni
  • 5:00 am: start on foot from Mvouni (about 370 m)
  • Rainforest, then high heathland
  • Arrival at the caldera around 11 am, break
  • Walk back down the same way, back in Moroni around 5:30 pm

Times are indicative and adjusted to the weather and the group’s pace.

Not included

  • Personal equipment (shoes, warm and rain clothes)
  • Backpack or walking poles rental: €5 each per trip
  • Personal insurance: not included, strongly recommended for visitors from abroad
  • Pick-up outside Moroni: supplement on quote
  • Personal expenses

Good to know

  • A booking is confirmed after talking with the team, never automatically.
  • The view from the top depends on the clouds: it is never guaranteed.
  • The 4×4 tours depend on the state of the track, checked the day before.
  • A trip may be adapted or postponed for safety reasons. The rules are in the terms of sale.
